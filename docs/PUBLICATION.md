## Data availability

The Source Data, reduced experimental measurements, frozen figure inputs and
eighteen prior-network checkpoints supporting this study are archived at
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24308307 (version 1.0.1). The deposit includes forty
selected raw waveforms and the inputs and code for the rank and covariance
sensitivity checks. Other raw time series are available from the corresponding
author on reasonable request.
